Write a reflection of 500 to 750 words on your work across the four music lessons. Use your Music Module Notes: the prompts you saved, the evaluation grids, and the reflection prompts you answered after each lesson.

## Prompts to address

Address all four; use headings so the reader can find each one.

1. **Growth.** Choose two of the seven competencies. For each, describe one specific moment in the module where you exercised it and what you would do differently now.
2. **The tool and you.** Where did the AI tools add something you could not have made alone, and where did your musicianship correct or override them? Give one concrete example of each.
3. **Ethics in practice.** Restate the classroom rule you proposed in Lesson 1.4. Has working through the whole module changed it? Why or why not?
4. **Transfer.** Pick one of the four artifacts. Describe how you would adapt the activity for a specific grade level and class you might teach, including one modification for a student with limited access to technology at home.

## Submission

Submit a PDF or a Google Doc link. Include your name, the module, and the date at the top. Write in first person. You may use a text model to check clarity, but the reflection must be your own writing; a reflection that reads as AI-generated will be returned unmarked.
