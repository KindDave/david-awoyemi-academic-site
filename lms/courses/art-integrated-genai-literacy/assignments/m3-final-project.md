Design a complete lesson plan that adapts one of your eight course artifacts for a specific class you might teach. Use the template on the "Designing an Arts-Integrated GenAI Lesson" page and fill every section.

## Requirements

- One lesson, 45 to 90 minutes, for a named grade band and class type.
- One arts standard and one technology or AI literacy standard.
- Two or three objectives with observable verbs, each labeled as an art outcome or a GenAI competency.
- A step-by-step activity that includes a "change one variable" cycle and an evaluation grid.
- A human-only step that the rubric assesses.
- A low-tech alternative path.
- An ethics moment.
- A rubric with three or four criteria.
- A one-page appendix: your own exemplar artifact for the lesson (this can be the course artifact you adapted) with its AI credit.

## Process

1. Draft the plan.
2. Post it in the Final Project Peer Feedback forum and give feedback on two peers' drafts using the rubric below.
3. Revise. Include a short "changes made from peer feedback" note at the end.
4. Submit as a PDF or a Google Doc link.

## Rubric (40 points)

| Criterion | Exemplary (10) | Proficient (8) | Developing (5) | Beginning (2) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Alignment | Standards, objectives, activity, and assessment line up; art outcome is primary and GenAI competency is explicit | Aligned with minor gaps | Objectives describe the tool rather than the learning, or assessment does not match objectives | Little alignment |
| Activity design | Clear steps; one-variable cycle; evaluation grid; human-only step is central and assessed | All elements present; one is weak | Missing one required element | Missing several |
| Equity and access | Low-tech path is realistic; differentiation is specific; data privacy is addressed | Low-tech path present; differentiation general | Low-tech path is a placeholder | Missing |
| Ethics and disclosure | Ethics moment is well placed and age-appropriate; credit line is required and modeled in the exemplar | Both present; one is general | One is missing | Both missing |
