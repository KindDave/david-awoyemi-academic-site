Write a reflection of 500 to 750 words on your work across the four theater lessons. Use your Theater Module Notes: the briefs, beat sheets, movement scores, failure logs, and the reflection prompts you answered after each lesson.

## Prompts to address

Address all four; use headings so the reader can find each one.

1. **Growth.** Choose two of the seven competencies. For each, describe one specific moment in the module where you exercised it and what you would do differently now.
2. **Back to the room.** Theater is live. For each of your four artifacts, write one sentence on how it would return to a rehearsal room with real students. Then pick the one with the strongest case and expand it to a paragraph.
3. **Feedback and judgment.** Using your Lesson 2.4 classification, describe how you will decide when to trust AI feedback on creative work, and how you will teach students to do the same.
4. **Position.** Restate your position on staging AI-assisted work. If you completed the music module, compare it with the classroom rule you set in Lesson 1.4. Are they consistent?

## Submission

Submit a PDF or a Google Doc link. Include your name, the module, and the date at the top. Write in first person. You may use a text model to check clarity, but the reflection must be your own writing; a reflection that reads as AI-generated will be returned unmarked.
