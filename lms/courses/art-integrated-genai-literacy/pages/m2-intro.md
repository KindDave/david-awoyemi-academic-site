## Before you start

- Complete the account setup for Animaker and invideo AI, and confirm access to a text model.
- Keep a running document titled "Theater Module Notes." Paste every prompt you use and one line about the result.
- If you completed the Music module, notice how often the same moves return: the five-part brief, one variable per iteration, and the six-criteria evaluation. Theater changes the vocabulary, not the method.

## A note on the live art

Theater happens between people in a room. Nothing here replaces that. The tools in this module help you draft faster, visualize staging before you have a cast, rehearse without a partner, and give students a way to see their ideas move. Every lesson asks how the artifact returns to the rehearsal room.

## Estimated time

Four to six hours across the four lessons. Each lesson takes 60 to 90 minutes including the gallery post and knowledge check.
