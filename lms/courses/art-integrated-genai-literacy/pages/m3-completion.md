## Take it further

- **Build your portfolio.** Choose your three strongest artifacts and write a one-paragraph teaching statement about GenAI in the arts to accompany them.
- **Run the lesson.** Teach your final project lesson in a practicum or with a volunteer group. Collect student artifacts (with permission) and reflect on what changed from plan to practice.
- **Join the conversation.** Professional organizations for music and theater educators now host AI interest groups and conference sessions. Bring your artifacts.
- **Keep reading.** Long and Magerko (2020) on AI literacy; UNESCO (2024) on AI competencies for teachers; the National Core Arts Standards for the arts outcomes your lessons serve.
