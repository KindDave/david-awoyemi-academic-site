Dive into sound creation, loop-based producing, beat making, and digital composition using GenAI tools. In this module you will learn how AI can amplify your musical creativity while you build the competencies that let you evaluate and guide it.

## What you will make

| Lesson | Artifact | Competencies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1.1 Composing an Original Song with GenAI | A 60 to 120 second song from your own lyrics | Prompt engineering, creativity and innovation |
| 1.2 Beat Making and Loop-Based Production | A 16-bar beat built from patterns you sequenced | Computational thinking, collaboration |
| 1.3 Scoring a Scene with AI-Generated Soundtracks | A scored 30 to 60 second scene with two contrasting cues | Problem solving, AI feedback evaluation |
| 1.4 Voice, Ownership, and Ethics in AI Music | An attribution statement and a position paragraph on a real case | AI ethics literacy, AI feedback evaluation |

## Before you start

- Complete the account setup for Suno and Soundtrap.
- Put on headphones. Many differences you will be asked to hear (tempo drift, muddy low end, robotic vocal phrasing) are hard to notice on laptop speakers.
- Keep a running document titled "Music Module Notes." Paste every prompt you use and one line about what it produced. You will need it for the module reflection.

## A note on musicianship

Nothing in this module replaces what you know about melody, harmony, rhythm, and form. The tools are fast and often impressive, and they are also frequently wrong about the things a trained musician notices first. Your expertise is what turns a generation into a composition. Every lesson asks you to bring that expertise to bear.

## Estimated time

Four to six hours across the four lessons. Each lesson takes 60 to 90 minutes including the gallery post and knowledge check.
