Write a case analysis of "Onboarding at Crestview Regional Hospital" using the six-step framework from the case method page. Your analysis should read as a memo Maya could hand to Dr. Vance on Monday.

## Requirements

- 1,500 to 2,000 words, with a heading for each of the six steps.
- Cite evidence from the case (paraphrase or quote what people said and what the data showed) at every step.
- Apply at least two concepts from Modules 1 and 2 by name (for example: needs assessment, the systems view, a specific ID model, formative evaluation, Merrill's principles).
- Propose at least three alternatives in step 4, including one non-instructional or partly instructional option.
- In step 5, include a two-week action plan.
- In step 6, name at least two early indicators you would monitor after go-live.
- APA 7 citations for any outside sources. An AI-use disclosure line if applicable.

## Submission

PDF or Google Doc link.
