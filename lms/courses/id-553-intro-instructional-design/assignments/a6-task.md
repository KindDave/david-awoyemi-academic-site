Conduct and report a task analysis for one instructional goal from your course design project.

## Requirements

1. **Goal statement.** One sentence describing what learners will be able to do, in what context, to what standard.
2. **Classification.** Classify the goal using Gagne's outcome types and justify the classification. If the goal combines types, say which dominates and where the others appear.
3. **Decomposition.** Use the method that matches the outcome type (procedural, hierarchical, cluster, or a combination) to at least three levels. Represent it as a flowchart, tree diagram, or structured table. Number every element. Include decisions, branch points, and common errors.
4. **Entry-skill line.** Mark where instruction begins, and justify the line with findings from your learner analysis (Assignment 5). If your learners split into subgroups with different entry skills, show both lines.
5. **Verification.** Have at least one person who performs the task review the analysis. Report what they added, removed, or corrected. If you used any cognitive task analysis technique (a narrated critical incident, a think-aloud), describe it.
6. **Reflection.** In one paragraph, describe what the analysis revealed that you would not have taught if you had designed from your own knowledge of the topic.

**Length.** The diagram or table plus 800 to 1,200 words of narrative.

## Submission

PDF or Google Doc link. Diagrams may be hand-drawn and photographed if legible. APA 7. AI-use disclosure if applicable.
