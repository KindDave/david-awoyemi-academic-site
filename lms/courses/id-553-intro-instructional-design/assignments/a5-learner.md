Conduct and report a learner and context analysis for your course design project.

## Requirements

**Data collection.** Use at least two methods (interviews, observation, survey, existing records, diagnostic task). Reach real learners or people who work closely with them. A small sample is acceptable; imagined learners are not. Describe your methods and sample in a short section.

**Learner analysis.** Address each category from the learner analysis page: entry skills, prior knowledge and misconceptions, attitudes toward content and delivery, motivation, educational and ability levels, learning preferences (as engagement information, not as a basis for matching), attitudes toward the organization, and group characteristics. For adult learners, add competing demands, relevant experience, and expected autonomy. Where you have no data for a category, say so and state what you would do to get it.

**Context analysis.** Describe the performance context and the learning context using the dimensions on the page, and assess the fit between them.

**Implications.** For at least eight findings, write a specific design implication in a two-column table (finding, implication). This table is the deliverable the rest of the course builds on.

**Length.** 1,200 to 1,800 words plus the implications table. Include your instruments (interview questions, survey items) in an appendix.

## Ethics

Tell participants what the information is for and that it is for a course project. Do not collect names or identifying details you do not need. If your learners are minors or you are collecting sensitive information, talk to the instructor first; institutional review may apply.

## Submission

PDF or Google Doc link. APA 7. AI-use disclosure if applicable.
