Revise your instructional plan in response to peer review and write a reflection on the design.

## Part A: Revised plan excerpt (20 points)

Submit the sections of your Part 1 plan that changed as a result of peer review and your own evaluation planning, with changes tracked or highlighted. Include a one-page change log: what changed, which review or insight prompted it, and what you chose not to change and why.

## Part B: Reflection (30 points)

Write 800 to 1,200 words addressing:

1. **The model in practice.** You chose a model or hybrid in Module 1. How well did it describe the process you actually followed? Where did you deviate, and was the deviation an improvement or a shortcut?
2. **Analysis to design.** Trace one line from a learner analysis finding through a task analysis element, an objective, an assessment, and a strategy decision. Did the chain hold, or did something break along the way?
3. **What the prototype and peer review taught you** that the design document did not.
4. **What you would do with more time, and what you would do with less.** Which components are essential and which are refinements?
5. **Your practice going forward.** One habit from this course you will keep and one you doubt.

## Submission

One PDF or Google Doc link. APA 7 where sources are cited. AI-use disclosure if applicable. The reflection must be your own writing.
