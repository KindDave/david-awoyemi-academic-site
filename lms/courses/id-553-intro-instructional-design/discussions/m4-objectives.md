The Objective Clinic is a peer workshop. Everyone brings objectives; everyone leaves with better ones.

**Initial post (by midweek).** Post three objectives from your task analysis: the terminal objective and two enabling objectives. Number each with its task analysis element. For the terminal objective, also post a draft assessment: the type, a description of the task or a sample item, and the scoring approach.

**Replies (by end of module).** Critique two classmates' sets. For each objective, check:

1. Is the verb observable and matched to the outcome type and level?
2. Are the conditions stated, and do they reflect what will be present at assessment and on the job?
3. Is the criterion a real standard, not "correctly" or "to the instructor's satisfaction"?
4. Is the draft assessment aligned on performance, conditions, and standard?

Rewrite at least one of their objectives as you would phrase it, and explain the change. Be direct and specific; this is a workshop, and vague praise helps nobody.

**Revision.** Edit your original post to add a "Revised" section with your improved objectives before the module closes. Graded on the quality of your critiques and the improvement in your revision (part of the discussions and knowledge checks component).
