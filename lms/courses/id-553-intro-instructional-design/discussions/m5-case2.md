The Pinewood case has more stakeholders with more conflicting interests than Crestview. Map them before you analyze.

**Initial post (by midweek).** Post a stakeholder table with at least seven rows: stakeholder, what they want, what they fear, and what they could contribute. Then answer in no more than 200 words: which two stakeholders' interests are most in conflict, and what design choice would you make to manage that conflict?

**Replies (by end of module).** Reply to two classmates. Identify one stakeholder or interest they missed or characterized differently from you, and say whether the difference would change the recommendation. Comment on whether their conflict-management choice is realistic given the budget and the eight-day deadline.
