Peer review is an expert-review stage of formative evaluation, and it is also practice in giving the kind of feedback designers receive from colleagues.

**Post (first three days of the module).** Attach or link your Assignment 7, Part 1 instructional plan and prototype. Title the thread with your project title. Add three sentences telling reviewers where you most want feedback.

**Review (by day seven).** You will be assigned two peers' plans (see the pairing announcement). For each, complete the peer review form:

1. **Alignment.** Check three objectives against their assessments and lessons. Report any mismatch specifically.
2. **Strategy.** Pick one lesson. Which of Gagne's nine events are strong, which are weak or missing, and what would you add?
3. **Multimedia and accessibility.** Name one Mayer principle the storyboard applies well and one it violates. Check the accessibility notes on three screens.
4. **Prototype.** Work through the prototype as a learner. Where did you hesitate or get confused? Could you perform the objective afterward?
5. **Two strengths, two priority revisions.** Be specific enough that the author can act without asking you what you meant.

Post the completed form as a reply in the author's thread. Reviews are graded as part of Assignment 7, Part 2. Authors respond to each review with one sentence acknowledging it and one sentence saying what they will change or why not.
