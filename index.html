Idowu David Awoyemi

Instructional Technology Researcher | AI and XR in Education | Learning Designer

Doctoral candidate in Instructional Technology with a distinguished record of integrating teaching excellence, impactful research, and transformative service to advance educational innovation and equity. Recognized as the Most Outstanding Graduate Student in Research (2025) and recipient of the Alabama Power Innovation and Technology Award (2025), with expertise in immersive learning technologies, AI-enhanced education, and broadening participation in STEM fields.

David studies immersive learning, AI-supported instruction, analytics, and equitable participation in STEM with a design orientation grounded in practical educational impact.
